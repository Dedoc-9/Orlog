### What survives the debate

Three ideas deserve further investigation:

1. Envelope checking: check a component with every slot it reads but does not write left free over the slot's declared domain.
2. May/must reasoning: an envelope can expose possible hazards, but a claimed gain requires a closed, replayable witness.
3. Interface-owned invariants: prove an invariant with every proposal left free; if it holds, components using that interface inherit the guarantee.

The judge's measured probe supports investigating these ideas in your actual mechanics. It does not establish that they work generally, or that the proposed Verðandi experiment will pass.&#x20;
